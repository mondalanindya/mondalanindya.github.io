Anindya Mondal

Third-year PhD student in Computer Vision and AI at the Surrey Institute for People-Centred AI (CVSSP), University of Surrey.

As a third-year PhD student, my research addresses challenges in representation learning for real-world settings by integrating auxiliary signals across modalities. I develop algorithms for vision–language integration focusing on robust action recognition, object counting, and text-to-image synthesis, with an emphasis on practical solutions for dynamic scene interpretation and generation.
